3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

To enhance your experience, our website utilizes "cookies" and similar tracking technologies. A cookie is a small text file placed on your hard disk by a web page server. Cookies cannot be used to run programs or deliver viruses to your computer.

  • Session Cookies: These are temporary and expire once you close your browser. They help our website remember your inputs on multi-step forms.
  • Persistent Cookies: These stay on your device until deleted. We use them to remember your preferences (such as ensuring you do not see the same popup form repeatedly).
  • Third-Party Analytics: We may use tools like Google Analytics to understand site traffic. These third parties may use their own cookies.

You have the ability to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser settings to decline them if you prefer. However, if you choose to decline cookies, some interactive features of our website may not function optimally.
